## Challenge — technology, problem and proposed solution

Rajasthan has already crossed the identity-creation threshold of AgriStack: 87.23 lakh Farmer IDs were reported as of 20 July 2026. The harder statewide problem is now keeping farmer-land-crop relationships continuously correct as land mutations, crop seasons, departmental records and service eligibility change. RAJ-AGRISETU X is Syntheon’s proposed post-registration operating layer for this problem. It does not create another master database. It federates existing authoritative systems through a temporal FarmGraph AI layer that links Farmer ID, Jan Aadhaar reference, land parcels, crop assertions, water context, consent, schemes and evidence with provenance and effective time. CARE - the Continuous Agricultural Reconciliation Engine - detects drift such as a sanctioned RoR mutation not yet reflected in Farmer Registry and converts it into an auditable human-authorised case instead of allowing AI to overwrite Government truth. SahmatiOS provides purpose-, scope- and duration-bound farmer consent aligned to AgriStack’s consent architecture and DPDP requirements. A deterministic Scheme Compiler translates authorised scheme rules into explainable readiness checks; AI may explain or flag anomalies but cannot grant statutory eligibility. The required last-mile channel is an installable Hindi-first offline PWA that can capture a farmer assertion locally, retain it through connectivity loss, receive a server acknowledgement on reconnect and route it to CARE for revalidation. Assisted eMitra/CSC/FPO/Krishi Sakhi workflows and Syntheon’s SUTRA-ID Edge are optional extensions for low-literacy and weak-connectivity contexts. The State Mission Control provides district readiness, reconciliation pendency, integration health, consent integrity, GIS context and audit evidence. The result is an end-to-end, interoperable AgriStack rollout framework that extends - rather than replaces - Farmer Registry/UFSI, e-Dharti/DILRMP, Jan Aadhaar, Raj Sewa Dwaar, Rajdharaa/LGD and approved Agriculture Department systems.

## Value proposition

Syntheon offers RAJ-AGRISETU X as a Government-ready AgriStack trust, reconciliation and service-delivery fabric. Its value is not another registration portal; it is an operating layer that makes Rajasthan’s existing agricultural DPI continuously useful after Farmer ID creation. The product combines six reusable capabilities. (1) FarmGraph AI creates a temporal, provenance-aware agricultural digital twin linking identity, land, crop, water, consent, schemes and evidence without duplicating authoritative masters. (2) CARE converts cross-system disagreement into bounded human-authorised workflows with source-state separation and decision receipts. (3) SahmatiOS gives farmers clear purpose, data scope, duration, revocation and language-aware notice, composing with AgriStack’s Consent Manager rather than creating a parallel consent protocol. (4) Scheme Compiler converts official rules into versioned deterministic checks, so AI explains decisions but never becomes the legal decision-maker. (5) The offline-first Kisan PWA supports self-verification, correction requests, scheme readiness and transactional offline sync; assisted-service and SUTRA-ID Edge channels reuse the same governed transaction contract. (6) State Mission Control gives nodal and district officers a real MIS for registry freshness, mutation-to-registry drift, CARE SLA, district rollout quality, offline reach, consent integrity, integration health and audit evidence. The architecture is designed to integrate through Rajasthan’s existing rails, especially Raj Sewa Dwaar, Jan Aadhaar 2.0, e-Dharti/DILRMP, Rajdharaa/LGD and Agriculture Department systems, while using UFSI/Sandbox patterns for AgriStack interoperability. The immediate commercial proposition is a milestone-gated 90-day production-core pilot for ₹72.50 lakh covering approved integration onboarding, security/UAT, three contrasting field contexts, training, documentation and statewide scale blueprint. Rajasthan gets measurable operational outcomes - faster reconciliation, fresher registries, fewer repeated farmer submissions, inclusive offline access and auditable service delivery - without replacing systems that already work.

## How the technology addresses the challenge

We would implement RAJ-AGRISETU X in five gated phases over 90 days, beginning with authority and interface discovery rather than prematurely centralising data. Days 0-15 establish the authoritative-source matrix across Agriculture, Revenue, Jan Aadhaar, Water Resources, Cooperatives and State middleware; map AgriStack/UFSI and Raj Sewa Dwaar interfaces; finalise pilot contexts, data-minimisation rules, security controls and synthetic test environments. Days 15-35 implement the trust core: FarmGraph temporal relationships, CARE reconciliation, consent-policy receipts, provenance/audit events and contract-tested adapters. Days 35-55 deliver the State/District Mission Control, installable offline Kisan PWA, deterministic Scheme Compiler, Hindi/BHASHINI-ready interactions and the optional SUTRA field execution pattern. Days 55-75 validate the same workflow in three contrasting Rajasthan contexts - an arid/low-connectivity setting, an irrigated/high-intensity agriculture setting and a high-mutation/peri-urban setting - selected with the Department. Days 75-90 complete security, performance, accessibility, offline-recovery and UAT gates; train users; hand over runbooks/API documentation; and deliver the phased statewide scale blueprint. The core rule is that authoritative records remain at their source. A RoR mutation or cross-system mismatch generates a CARE case; the farmer may confirm or dispute through the PWA/assisted channel, even offline; reconnect produces an acknowledgement, not approval; an authorised officer compares source facts, farmer assertion and AI observation; only the approved Government workflow can propagate the final state. Consent is purpose-bound and revocable. Scheme readiness is deterministic and source-traceable. AI is limited to translation, OCR, anomaly detection, entity matching, explanation and prioritisation; it cannot alter RoR/Farmer ID, establish a legal crop fact or approve/deny a benefit. Success will be measured through mutation-to-CARE detection time, CARE resolution SLA, registry freshness, offline acknowledgement rate, purpose-consent completeness, API/retry health and reduction in repeated data/document submission. This directly meets the challenge’s requirements for land/Jan Aadhaar linkage, IDEA/UFSI-aligned exchange, DPDP-aligned consent, offline self-verification and scheme linkage while fitting Rajasthan’s existing digital architecture.

## Available resource / USP

RAJ-AGRISETU X transcends conventional registry portals, API dashboards and generic “AI agriculture” proposals in four ways. First, it attacks the post-registration failure mode: relationships become stale when land, cultivation, crop or service context changes. FarmGraph AI therefore models agricultural reality as a temporal graph whose edges carry source authority, effective time, verification state, consent purpose and evidence provenance. This enables precise drift detection without creating another master database. Second, CARE makes disagreement a governed workflow. Source facts, farmer assertions, AI observations and official decisions are never collapsed into one field; AI can detect and explain a conflict but an authorised human must resolve consequential state. Third, offline operation is transactional rather than cosmetic. The farmer PWA stores a bounded action locally, survives connectivity loss, receives an acknowledgement on reconnect and routes the item for revalidation; acknowledgement never masquerades as Government approval. Fourth, integration maturity is labelled honestly as live prototype, sandbox, public reference, contract-ready or adapter pattern, eliminating “fake green API” theatre. Syntheon also brings unusual execution assets for a young startup: a working SUTRA-ID Edge handheld capability for standalone voice/touch/evidence workflows in low-connectivity environments; governed-AI and evidence-linked workflow experience across our public-sector challenge projects; and an engineering approach centred on deterministic authority boundaries, signed receipts and auditability. The prototype already demonstrates Mission Control, FarmGraph AI, CARE decision receipts, purpose-consent logic, Scheme Compiler traces, an installable offline farmer PWA and deterministic evaluator APIs. The proposed 90-day engagement is therefore not funding a slideware concept: it converts a de-risked prototype into a Department-approved production core using Rajasthan’s existing DPI. Our defensibility is the combined operating model - temporal trust graph + human reconciliation + purpose consent + offline transactions + deterministic service rules - which can later support insurance, procurement, input distribution, dealer systems, DaaS and verified agricultural claims through the same governed infrastructure.
