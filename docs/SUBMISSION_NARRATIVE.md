# Submission Narrative Spine

**Problem:** Rajasthan has achieved Farmer-ID scale, but statewide value depends on keeping farmer-land-crop relationships fresh, resolving interdepartmental conflicts, obtaining understandable purpose consent, and serving farmers who cannot reliably transact online.

**Resolution:** RAJ-AGRISETU X adds a federated operating layer: CARE reconciliation, purpose-policy consent, deterministic scheme pre-checks, officer pendency/MIS, and transactional offline farmer access.

**Rajasthan fit:** The architecture extends—not replaces—e-Dharti/DILRMP, Jan Aadhaar, Raj Sewa Dwaar, Raj SSO, RajMasters, Rajdharaa, RajKisan and central AgriStack/UFSI rails.

**Last mile:** Installable farmer PWA is the primary offline requirement; assisted services and SUTRA-ID Edge provide inclusive fallback channels. BHASHINI is the preferred language rail where authorized.

**Safety:** AI is advisory/explanatory. Land, registry and statutory benefit state remain under authoritative/human control.

**90-day result:** a production-core, approved integration foundation and field-validated pilot with measurable reconciliation, offline and consent KPIs—not a misleading promise of instant statewide replacement.
