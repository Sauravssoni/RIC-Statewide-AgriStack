# Business / Procurement Positioning

## 90-day engagement
Indicative prototype proposal: ₹72.50 lakh for production-core implementation, approved integration onboarding, field pilot, UAT, documentation and handover. Final commercials remain subject to departmental scope, approved hosting/security requirements and Government procurement terms.

## Value delivered to Rajasthan
- protect Farmer Registry freshness as land relationships change
- reduce manual cross-system mismatch resolution
- make farmer consent understandable and auditable
- provide a real offline transaction path for last-mile service
- reduce repeated submission of information already held by authoritative Government systems
- give State/district teams operational visibility into rollout quality rather than registration counts alone

## Scale model
State implementation + O&M/support + approved new departmental adapters + field-channel support. Future reuse for other States is architecture/product reuse, not monetization of farmer personal data.

**Commercial principle:** monetize safe interoperability and service infrastructure, never farmer personal data.
