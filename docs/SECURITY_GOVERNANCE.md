# Security, Privacy & Responsible-AI Design

## Core controls
- federated data model; no unnecessary central replication;
- data minimization and purpose-scoped claims;
- role-based access and policy scopes;
- encryption in transit/at rest in production deployment;
- source provenance and signed/tamper-evident event receipts;
- consent purpose, data category, duration and revocation metadata;
- offline queue integrity with source snapshot hash and replay protection design;
- explicit production secrets management; no credentials in repository;
- auditable human authorization for authoritative changes.

## AI boundary
AI can translate, explain, OCR, summarize, flag anomalies, assist entity matching and recommend next actions. AI cannot autonomously mutate RoR, change Farmer IDs, establish authoritative crop facts, approve/deny statutory benefits or override authorized officials.

## Privacy statement
SahmatiOS is described as **DPDP-aligned/future-ready consent architecture**, not as a claim that Syntheon is a statutory registered Consent Manager. Production legal roles, notices, retention, grievance, security safeguards and any Consent Manager integration would be finalized with the Government of Rajasthan and applicable law/rules.
