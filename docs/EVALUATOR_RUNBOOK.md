## Judge questions answered proactively
- **Why not another Farmer Registry?** Rajasthan already has Farmer-ID scale. This project operationalizes freshness, reconciliation, consent and service execution around the federated State-owned registry.
- **Does AI change land or eligibility?** No. AI observes/explains/prioritizes. Authorized humans and source systems control governed state.
- **Is offline just cached HTML?** No. It is transactional: local queue → ACK → CARE revalidation → human decision → authorized adapter.
- **Are Government APIs really live?** Only genuinely available prototype/sandbox/public-reference states are labelled as such. Contract-ready is not represented as production-connected.
- **Why FarmGraph AI?** It gives Rajasthan a temporal, provenance-aware digital twin of agricultural relationships without creating a duplicate master database.
- **Why SUTRA?** Optional voice/low-connectivity field rail for last-mile access; it does not replace the farmer PWA.
