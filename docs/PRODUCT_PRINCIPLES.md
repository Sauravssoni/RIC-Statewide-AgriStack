# Product Principles

1. **Federated before centralized.** Keep authoritative data with its authoritative system.
2. **Reconcile before overwrite.** Conflicts become CARE cases, not silent database fixes.
3. **Human authority before autonomous decision.** AI explains and prioritizes; authorized officials decide governed state.
4. **Offline is transactional.** A useful offline app can capture intent/evidence and later obtain a server acknowledgement.
5. **Consent must be understandable.** Purpose, data scope, duration, modality and revocation are explicit.
6. **Verified facts and demo KPIs never mix.** Evidence labels are part of the UX.
7. **Existing Rajasthan rails first.** Raj Sewa Dwaar, Raj SSO, Jan Aadhaar, e-Dharti, Rajdharaa, RajMasters and RajKisan are integration anchors, not systems to replace.
8. **One workflow, many channels.** Farmer PWA, assisted service and SUTRA-ID Edge use compatible governed transaction contracts.
9. **Map is context, not truth theatre.** Production geometry comes from authorized Rajdharaa/LGD services.
10. **Procurement proof over feature count.** Every screen should answer an operational Government question.
